Professional Profile

Dr. Niraj K. Nagrale is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Electronics & Telecommunication Engineering at G H Raisoni College of Engineering, Nagpur.


His academic and research interests include Artificial Intelligence, Deep Learning, Ear Biometrics, Image Processing, Digital Signal Processing, Computer Vision and Internet of Things.
