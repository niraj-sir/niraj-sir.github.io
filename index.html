About Me

Academic Profile & Professional Journey

Professional Profile

Dr. Niraj K. Nagrale is an experienced academician in Electronics and Telecommunication Engineering with extensive experience in undergraduate and postgraduate teaching, laboratory instruction, student mentoring and academic administration.


His research interests include Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Digital Signal Processing, Digital Image Processing, Ear Biometrics, Computer Vision, IoT and Soft Computing.

Academic Philosophy

His academic work integrates theoretical engineering concepts with emerging technologies and practical applications. He actively mentors students in research and project development and contributes to curriculum development, professional activities and research.


He has also contributed to academic coordination, student affairs, professional bodies and conference reviewing activities.
